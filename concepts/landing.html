GOOD TO KNOW

A couple of practical details.

Why does push need HTTPS?

Browsers require a secure HTTPS connection for push notifications. Open the self-hosted tracker over HTTPS to enable push; email reminders are a separate option.

Where does the tracker run?

It is intended to run on Lachie’s own server or machine. It is a personal tool with no login flow.

What can it monitor?

Public websites’ SSL certificates and domain registration expiry dates via RDAP.
