# certwatch — concept short

## Concept
**The dates you don't have to keep in your head.**

A pair of quiet calendar slips—SSL certificate and domain registration—become a clear dashboard, then configurable email and browser push reminders. The ending returns to the established green checkmark and certwatch name.

## Feel
Quiet confidence, pale sage and established certwatch greens, concise UI-led copy, gentle motion, and a warm restrained sound bed.

## Product truth
The app checks certificate and registration expiry dates daily, with registration lookup support depending on RDAP publication. Users can set reminder thresholds and email/push channels. Email delivery depends on server configuration; browser push needs HTTPS or localhost and browser permission. The film avoids automatic-renewal and uptime claims.
