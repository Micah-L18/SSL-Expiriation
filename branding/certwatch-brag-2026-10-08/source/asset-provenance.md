# Editable source and provenance

- `../composition/index.html` is the editable HyperFrames production source: original scene layout, brand-aligned motion, synthetic interface states, audio placements, and product copy.
- `certwatch-style.css` is a copy of the product's existing stylesheet used as a direct palette, typography, and UI reference.
- The dashboard and alert mockups in the composition are recreated from `app/public/index.html` and the existing CSS; they use fictional `.example` domain names, dates, and status counts. They are not connected to the app or its data store.
- No production database, push key file, secrets, email addresses, or personal information is used.
- Music and interface SFX are copied from Brag's bundled asset library; the composition references only the local copies under `composition/assets/`.
